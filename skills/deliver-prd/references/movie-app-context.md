## Check project-specific product decisions

Reconcile the following with current approved scope; apply them only where still established. Do not turn an older recommendation into a commitment silently.

- **Users and permissions:** Visitor, signed-in Viewer, and Maintainer are application users. Maintainers retain catalog-reading capabilities alongside management; the current policy gives Maintainers the same management permissions. Developer, assessment reviewer, and operator are delivery responsibilities, not extra personas. A connected MCP assistant acts as a tool under a Maintainer's permissions.
- **Accounts:** Current scope includes public name/email/password registration into Viewer access and prepared Viewer/Maintainer demo accounts. Email verification, password recovery, role-management screens, and public account directories remain excluded unless explicitly added later.
- **Catalog and seed:** Preserve the commitment to exactly 100 distinct movies and 100 distinct TV series in a fresh initial dataset, provenance, varied release years/known months, and honest date precision. Ordinary reruns preserve user edits, archive states, permanent deletions, and removed credits. TV browsing/details/search/filtering are included; interactive TV, season, and episode management remain excluded.
- **Discovery and information:** Search partial display/original titles and person names, including cast and creators, without case sensitivity; exclude account data. Multiple genres match any selected genre while type, year/month, and text filters narrow together; month requires year and clearing search preserves other filters. Preserve selected ordering and partial-date rules. Manually created movies need title, synopsis, genre, and release year; optional metadata may be unknown.
- **Terminology and lifecycle:** Explain that a credit links a shared person to a title and role, grouped as Cast or Creator. Current roles include Actor, Director, Writer, Producer, and custom roles, with multiple roles/character names as established. Current approved removal is archive-first for movies and people, then restore or separately confirmed permanent deletion. Person archiving retains relationships privately; restoration reveals only surviving credits on active titles. Preserve impact previews, shared-person/title survival, archive visibility, and the rule that restoration never recreates deleted people or removed credits. This policy is specific to this project.
- **Edits and artwork:** Reject stale saves while retaining a reviewable draft. Distinguish uploading, processing, saved, failed, and uncertain results; failed replacement retains current artwork. Explain user recovery without prescribing transport, storage, or worker design.
- **AI creation:** Current MCP scope permits proposed new movies through ChatGPT and Claude, reviewed/corrected/rejected in chat by the connected Maintainer. Approval is explicit human approval of exact reviewed content; changed proposals need fresh approval, AI assertions are insufficient, and the proposing Maintainer may approve without a second person. Invalid/rejected proposals remain unpublished. Duplicate and uncertain submissions must not produce unintended extra movies or claim unconfirmed success. AI editing, deletion, account management, and artwork manipulation remain excluded.
- **Audit and privacy:** Current scope includes read-only Maintainer audit history with attributable actor, action, affected record, time, outcome, and relevant changes; it is not arbitrary restoration. Protect private account data from public views, search, unauthorized access, logs, and AI proposals. Preserve the selected AES-256-GCM/key-rotation constraint independently of private example links; passwords require one-way hashing. Encryption internals belong in technical design.
- **Input policy:** Current selected configurable name defaults are catalog person 1 to 100 and registration name 2 to 100 Unicode code points, with single name fields and legitimate mononyms/punctuation. They are app selections, not universal name coverage; acknowledge excluded one-character registration names and unmeasured distributions. Other bounds remain Recommended where not adopted. Current supported artwork formats are static JPEG, PNG, and WebP with a configurable supported subset; 1 byte through 5 MiB (5,242,880 bytes) is a recommended size range. Preserve content-validity checks, correction feedback, optional clearing/omission, and compatibility after changed limits. Verify current sources/statuses before repeating values. Unrelated products receive their own policies and evidence.
- **Deferred product direction:** Ratings/reviews, review aggregation, journaling, critic personalization, and detailed season/episode tracking stay outside this delivery unless a newer instruction changes their status.
