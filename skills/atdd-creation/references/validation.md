## Invocation and assurance limits

Instruction-level positive trigger checks cover creating an acceptance specification, updating one after source changes, and deriving engineering scenarios from supplied requirements. Research-only/skill-setup requests and runtime/TDD implementation requests do not themselves authorize this document generation. Loader metadata was validated; no fresh-session automatic-discovery harness was available to prove future model routing.

The evaluations are bounded examples, not evidence of exhaustive coverage or universal model compliance. Each author did its own initial reader pass; the parent supplied the separate semantic/presentation review. The final targeted repairs were rechecked, not presented as a new blinded full-generation trial. Semantic completeness, source-inventory completeness and authentic execution evidence remain review responsibilities beyond the deterministic helper.

Staging retains source identities, initial and final evaluation artifacts, checker reports, review findings, presentation evidence and a file-hash installation manifest. The real application's ATDD is deliberately deferred to a later invocation. Existing TDD workflows are not modified by this bundle.
