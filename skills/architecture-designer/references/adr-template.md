# Embedded architectural decisions

Use stable decision IDs, status, source context, decision drivers, selected option, viable alternatives, consequences, affected contracts and verification. Record why alternatives do not meet this project's constraints rather than repeating generic product comparisons.

Follow the parent's output policy. For a one-document design keep a decision index and anchored entries in that file; there is no separate ADR directory. Preserve IDs and supersession history on updates. Accepted design is distinct from implemented or tested behavior.
